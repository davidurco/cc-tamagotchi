# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-02)

The first release.

- An egg that hatches once Claude gets to work, then grows from a Bit into a Nibble, a teen
  (Bytey or Glitchy) and one of eight adults, picked by how it was cared for and what kind of
  work it watched Claude do.
- Hunger, fun, discipline and weight; naps when nothing is going on; bugs it leaves behind;
  sickness, tantrums, care mistakes, and (in classic mode) leaving for good when badly neglected.
- It eats Claude's work: edits, passing and failing tests, commits and pushes.
- `/tama`: the device in a pane, with an animated pixel LCD in the terminal (half blocks, doubled
  where there is room) and an animated egg-shaped device as SVG in the desktop app.
- A band above the prompt: the pet strolling in braille dots while Claude works, one line
  otherwise. Its face beside the spinner, its needs on the hint line under the prompt.
- The left-or-right game, six shell colors, a hall of fame, vacation mode, optional beeps.
- Zero tokens: nothing reaches the model.
